F1 Walk-Forward Evaluation - Plackett-Luce Bayesian Update
=======================================================

Method: 4-model BMA base (XGB+Ranker+RF+ET, pre-race features)
        + Bayesian Plackett-Luce within-season updating
        SIGMA2_BASE=2.0, DECAY_LAMBDA=0.1, ALPHA_CAP=0.45, WINNER_WEIGHT=2.0, QUAL_PL_WEIGHT=0.25, TAIL_WEIGHT=0.7
        Blend: inv-variance × data-sufficiency × entropy × dominance-gap × grid-alignment
        + dual PL (season + recent-window) with transition detection
        + team-level PL for car-development propagation
